**Business Associate:** Prizm, a Montana limited liability company ("Prizm")

---

## RECITALS

Practice is a Covered Entity as defined under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 and its implementing regulations ("HIPAA"). Prizm provides software services to Practice that involve the creation, receipt, maintenance, or transmission of Protected Health Information ("PHI"). The parties enter into this Agreement to comply with the requirements of HIPAA, the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health Act ("HITECH"), and all applicable regulations.

---

## ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Breach** has the meaning given under 45 CFR § 164.402.

1.2 **Business Associate** means Prizm, as defined under 45 CFR § 160.103.

1.3 **Covered Entity** means Practice, as defined under 45 CFR § 160.103.

1.4 **Protected Health Information (PHI)** has the meaning given under 45 CFR § 160.103, limited to PHI that Prizm creates, receives, maintains, or transmits on behalf of Practice.

1.5 **Required by Law** has the meaning given under 45 CFR § 164.103.

1.6 **Secretary** means the Secretary of the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services.

1.7 **Security Incident** has the meaning given under 45 CFR § 164.304.

1.8 **Subcontractor** means any person or entity to whom Prizm delegates a function, activity, or service involving PHI.

---

## ARTICLE 2 — PERMITTED USES AND DISCLOSURES

2.1 **Permitted Uses.** Prizm may use PHI only as necessary to perform the services described in the Service Agreement between the parties, including:
- Verifying patient insurance eligibility
- Generating personalized patient outreach campaigns
- Delivering SMS and email communications to patients on behalf of Practice
- Storing patient records necessary to provide the service

2.2 **Permitted Disclosures.** Prizm may disclose PHI:
- As necessary to perform services under the Service Agreement
- As Required by Law
- For proper management and administration of Prizm's business, provided that disclosures are Required by Law or Prizm obtains reasonable assurances the PHI will be held confidentially

2.3 **Prohibited Uses.** Prizm shall not:
- Use or disclose PHI in a manner that violates HIPAA
- Use PHI for Prizm's own marketing purposes
- Sell PHI to any third party
- Use PHI in any way not authorized by this Agreement or required by law

---

## ARTICLE 3 — PRIZM'S OBLIGATIONS

3.1 **Safeguards.** Prizm shall implement appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of PHI, in accordance with 45 CFR Part 164, Subpart C.

3.2 **Reporting.** Prizm shall report to Practice:
- Any Breach of Unsecured PHI within 60 days of discovery, in accordance with 45 CFR § 164.410
- Any Security Incident of which it becomes aware, without unreasonable delay
- Any use or disclosure of PHI not provided for by this Agreement, without unreasonable delay

3.3 **Subcontractors.** Prizm shall ensure any Subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on behalf of Prizm agrees to restrictions and conditions at least as stringent as those in this Agreement.

3.4 **Access to PHI.** Prizm shall make PHI available to Practice as necessary for Practice to fulfill its obligations to provide individuals with access to their PHI under 45 CFR § 164.524.

3.5 **Amendments.** Prizm shall make PHI available for amendment and incorporate any amendments requested by Practice in accordance with 45 CFR § 164.526.

3.6 **Accounting.** Prizm shall maintain and make available the information required to provide an accounting of disclosures in accordance with 45 CFR § 164.528.

3.7 **Compliance Audit.** Prizm shall make its internal practices, books, and records relating to the use and disclosure of PHI available to the Secretary for purposes of determining HIPAA compliance.

3.8 **Minimum Necessary.** Prizm shall use, disclose, or request only the minimum amount of PHI necessary to accomplish the intended purpose.

---

## ARTICLE 4 — PRACTICE'S OBLIGATIONS

4.1 Practice shall notify Prizm of any restriction on the use or disclosure of PHI that Practice has agreed to or is required to abide by.

4.2 Practice shall not request Prizm to use or disclose PHI in any manner that would violate HIPAA if done by Practice.

4.3 Practice shall obtain any consent or authorization required by applicable law prior to transmitting PHI to Prizm.

4.4 Practice is responsible for ensuring that patients whose data is uploaded have been properly informed of Practice's privacy practices.

---

## ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 **Term.** This Agreement shall remain in effect for the duration of the Service Agreement between the parties, unless terminated earlier.

5.2 **Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party materially breaches any provision of this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within 30 days of receiving written notice of the breach.

5.3 **Effect of Termination.** Upon termination:
- Prizm shall return or destroy all PHI received from or created on behalf of Practice, if feasible
- If return or destruction is not feasible, Prizm shall extend the protections of this Agreement to the PHI and limit further uses and disclosures to those purposes that make return or destruction infeasible
- Prizm shall certify in writing to Practice that PHI has been returned or destroyed

---

## ARTICLE 6 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

6.1 **Amendment.** The parties agree to amend this Agreement to the extent necessary to comply with changes in HIPAA, HITECH, or applicable regulations.

6.2 **Interpretation.** This Agreement shall be interpreted as broadly as necessary to implement and comply with HIPAA and HITECH.

6.3 **Survival.** The obligations of Prizm under Article 5.3 shall survive termination of this Agreement.

6.4 **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Utah.

6.5 **Entire Agreement.** Together with the Service Agreement, this Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the subject matter herein and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements.
