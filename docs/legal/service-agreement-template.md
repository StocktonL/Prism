## 1. SERVICES

1.1 **Description.** Prizm provides a cloud-based software platform for independent optometry practices that enables:
- Patient insurance eligibility verification
- Automated patient outreach campaign generation
- AI-personalized SMS and email campaign delivery
- Insurance benefit tracking and reporting

1.2 **Access.** Upon execution of this Agreement and receipt of payment, Prizm will provide Client with access credentials to the Prizm platform.

1.3 **Support.** Prizm will provide email support via stockton@prizmvision.com. Prizm will respond to support requests within one business day.

☐ **Standard Rate:** $449.00 USD per month

☐ **Founding Customer Rate:** $199.00 USD per month (lifetime rate, available to first 10 customers only, requires annual commitment)

2.2 **Billing.** Client authorizes Prizm to charge the monthly fee to the payment method on file on the same date each month beginning on the Effective Date.

## 3. TERM AND CANCELLATION

3.1 **Standard Rate Term.** Month-to-month. Either party may cancel with 30 days written notice.

3.2 **Founding Customer Term.** Annual commitment required. Client may cancel at the end of each annual term with 30 days written notice. Early cancellation during an annual term forfeits the founding rate — Client may continue at the standard rate of $449/month on a month-to-month basis.

3.3 **Effect of Cancellation.** Upon cancellation, Client's access to the platform will be terminated at the end of the paid period. Prizm will make Client's data available for export for 30 days following termination.

## 4. DATA AND PRIVACY

4.1 **Client Data Ownership.** Client retains ownership of all patient data uploaded to the Prizm platform. Prizm does not claim any ownership rights over Client's data.

4.2 **BAA Required.** Client and Prizm must execute a Business Associate Agreement ("BAA") prior to Client uploading any patient data to the platform. The BAA is incorporated into and made part of this Agreement.

4.3 **Data Security.** Prizm maintains administrative, physical, and technical safeguards appropriate to protect Client data, including HIPAA-compliant data storage and transmission.

4.4 **Data Return.** Upon termination, Prizm will provide Client with an export of their patient data in CSV format within 30 days of the termination date, at no additional charge.

4.5 **No Sale of Data.** Prizm will not sell, rent, or share Client's patient data with any third party except as required to provide the services (e.g., SMS delivery providers) or as required by law.
