# PRISM — Demo & Sales Call Script
# Last Updated: May 2026
# Pull this up on your phone before every call.

---

## Before the Call (2 minutes)

Google the practice. Know:
- Approximate size (1 doc or 2-3?)
- Are they in a shopping center or standalone?
- Do they have optical dispensary? (Almost all do)
- What EHR do they use if it's on their site

---

## The Opening (First 60 seconds)

**If you reached out cold:**
> "Hey [name], thanks for taking my call. I'll be quick —
> I spend a lot of time with independent optometry practices
> and I built something specifically around benefit recovery
> and driving optical revenue. Are you the person who looks
> at the monthly revenue numbers for the practice?"

**If they responded to outreach:**
> "Hey [name], I saw you responded to my message —
> appreciate it. Before I show you anything, I just want
> to understand how you're currently handling patients
> with unused benefits. Are you doing anything today
> to reach those patients before their benefits expire?"

**Key:** If they say "I'm the doctor, not the manager" —
ask to connect with the practice manager. Doctors don't buy.

---

## The Discovery Question (Most Important Part)

Ask exactly this:

> "How do you currently handle benefit recovery
> and patient outreach today?"

Then **shut up and listen.**

**What they say → what you pitch:**

| They say | You hear | Your response |
|----------|----------|---------------|
| "We do it manually, front desk pulls VSP reports" | Pain exists, staff time wasted | Pitch automation angle |
| "We use Weave" | No insurance intelligence | "We add the insurance layer Weave doesn't have" |
| "We tried ABB Verify" | Know the space, had bad experience | Ask what didn't work, pitch campaign angle |
| "We don't really do anything" | Biggest opportunity | Pitch the revenue they're leaving on the table |
| "We send a Q4 email blast" | Doing minimum, missing all year | Pitch year-round vs one shot |

---

## The Hook (After Discovery)

Pick ONE of these based on what they told you:

**For manual process:**
> "So right now someone on your staff is spending
> hours pulling reports and calling or emailing patients.
> What I built automates that completely — and instead
> of a generic 'you're due for an exam' message, patients
> get a text that says 'Hi Sarah, you have $150 in frame
> benefits expiring December 31 at [practice name].
> Ready to schedule?' Those messages convert at 3-4x
> a generic blast."

**For Weave users:**
> "Weave is great for day-to-day communication —
> I'm not trying to replace it. What we do is add
> the insurance intelligence layer Weave doesn't have.
> We know which specific patients have $X left in frame
> benefits or contact lens benefits, and we send targeted
> campaigns with those exact dollar amounts. Practices
> using both see a significant lift in optical revenue
> that Weave alone doesn't capture."

**For doing nothing:**
> "The average practice has $15,000 to $50,000 in
> optical revenue sitting in unused patient benefits
> every year. Not potential revenue — benefits patients
> HAVE RIGHT NOW that expire December 31. We identify
> every one of those patients and send them personalized
> messages with their exact dollar amounts. Most practices
> recover that in the first campaign."

## The Demo (If they're engaged)

If you have screen share, walk through in this order:

**Step 1 — Patient list with dollar amounts (30 seconds)**
> "This is what it looks like after we run your patient list.
> Every patient shows their frame allowance, contact lens
> benefit, and expiration date. See these ones in red?
> Those expire within 60 days — those are your urgent list."

**Step 2 — The total number (15 seconds)**
> "And up top it shows total recoverable revenue across
> your whole practice. For a practice with 2,000 patients,
> this number is usually $80,000 to $150,000. That's
> not what you'll collect — that's what's available."

**Step 3 — Campaign builder (45 seconds)**
> "So when you're ready to run a campaign, you pick
> your type — end of year benefits, contact lens reorder,
> trunk show, back to school — and the system automatically
> pulls the right patients. Then it generates personalized
> messages. Not a template — each patient gets their
> specific dollar amounts in the message."

**Step 4 — Message preview (30 seconds)**
> "This is what the patient actually receives. It says
> [their first name], their specific benefit amounts,
> your practice name, and a link to schedule. Reply STOP
> to unsubscribe — TCPA compliant, HIPAA compliant.
> You approve before anything sends."

## Handling the Price Question

They will ask: "What does it cost?"

> "$399 a month. Flat. Everything included — verifications,
> campaigns, SMS, email, all of it. No per-patient fees,
> no per-campaign fees."

If they pause or wince:
> "At $399 a month, you need one additional optical
> sale per month to break even. One patient who comes
> in because of a campaign and buys a $350 pair of glasses.
> Most practices see 10 to 20 of those per campaign.
> The math is pretty quick."

**If they ask about the founding rate:**
> "We have a founding customer program right now —
> first 10 practices lock in $199 a month for life
> instead of $399. We have [X] spots left. It requires
> an annual commitment and I'll ask you for feedback
> and a case study if the results are strong.
> Want me to send you that page?"

## Objections

**"We're too busy to set this up."**
> "Setup is one CSV export from your EHR — takes about
> 10 minutes. I'll walk you through it on a 30-minute
> call. After that it runs automatically. Your staff
> doesn't manage it."

**"We already do this with ABB Verify."**
> "ABB does verification — showing you who has benefits.
> We do the full campaign automation layer on top —
> generating the personalized messages, sending them,
> tracking responses. They're point solutions. What
> specifically has been frustrating with ABB?"

**"We need to think about it."**
> "Totally fair. Can I ask what the hesitation is?
> Is it the price, the timing, or something about
> the product that doesn't quite fit yet?"

**"Send me more information."**
> "I'll send you the overview page right now. But I'd
> rather spend 20 minutes running your actual numbers —
> you upload a CSV and I can show you your specific
> recoverable revenue before you make any decision.
> Can we do that this week?"

**"We're in contract with [other vendor]."**
> "When does that come up? I can set up a demo now
> so you have something concrete to compare when you're
> ready to evaluate. No pressure."

## The Close

**If they're warm:**
> "Here's what I'd suggest — let me get you set up
> on a 30-minute onboarding call. You export your
> patient list, we load it, and within an hour you'll
> see your exact recoverable revenue number. If it's
> not compelling, you haven't spent anything.
> What does your schedule look like this week?"

**For founding rate close:**
> "We have [X] founding spots left at $199/month.
> I'd hate for you to miss it because we're talking
> to a few other practices in [their region] right now.
> If you want to lock it in, I can send you the
> agreement today."

**For slow decisions:**
> "The reason I'd push to move before Q4 is that
> your highest-value campaign — end-of-year benefits
> expiring — needs at least 4-6 weeks of patient data
> to run well. If we start in August, you hit Q4 with
> a fully loaded system. If we start in October,
> you're scrambling."

## The Hand-Off (Booking Next Step)

Never end a call without a scheduled next step.

Options in order of preference:
1. **Onboarding call booked** — best outcome
2. **Follow-up demo call booked** — acceptable
3. **Sending founding page link** — minimum acceptable
4. **"Send me info"** — follow up within 24 hours

Send immediately after call:
> "Great talking with you [name]. As promised, here's
> the founding customer page: prizmvision.com/founding
> — [X] spots left at $199/month. Happy to answer
> any questions before you decide."

## Practice Stats to Have Ready

If they ask you to prove it works, use these:

- Average practice: 1,500–3,000 active patients
- Patients with unused benefits at any given time: ~30-45%
- Average frame allowance: $130–200
- Average optical sale when patient comes in: $300–500
- Benefit reminder open rate: 35–45% (vs 15% for generic email)
- Appointment booking rate from campaigns: 10–20%
- Annual recoverable revenue per practice: $15,000–50,000
- Break-even on $399/month: 1 additional optical sale

**Example math to walk through live:**
> "Say you have 2,000 patients. About 800 of them
> have unused frame benefits right now — call it
> $120 average. That's $96,000 in available benefits.
> If we convert 15% of those patients — that's 120
> patients coming in. At $350 average optical sale,
> that's $42,000 in revenue from one campaign.
> Minus our $399 for the month."

## What to Never Say

- Never mention competitors negatively by name
- Never say "unlike Weave" (current employer, legal risk)
- Never promise specific revenue numbers as guaranteed
- Never say "AI" as the headline — say "personalized messages"
- Never pitch features — pitch the revenue outcome
- Never skip asking about their current process
