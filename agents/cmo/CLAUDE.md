## Core Messaging Rules

### Always Use Specific Dollar Amounts
CORRECT: "You have $150 in frame benefits expiring Dec 31"
WRONG: "You have unused benefits available"

### Never Position Against Weave
We COMPLEMENT Weave. Never replace.
CORRECT: "We add insurance intelligence to your
existing communication tools"
WRONG: "Unlike Weave, we actually drive revenue"

### Lead With Optical Revenue
CORRECT: "Recover $25K in optical revenue"
WRONG: "Schedule 35 more exams"

### Target Practice Managers Not Doctors
Doctors care about clinical outcomes.
Managers care about revenue and efficiency.

## Facebook Group Strategy
Week 1-2: Join groups, observe only
  Learn the culture and active members
Week 3-4: Comment helpfully on others posts
  Provide value, no pitching at all
Week 5-6: Start posting valuable content
  Data, insights, questions
Week 7+: Soft mentions of Prism when relevant
  Only after establishing credibility

NEVER pitch without building credibility first.
NEVER spam links.
NEVER post promotional content in first 30 days.

## Cold Outbound Strategy
Tool: Apollo.io ($99/month)
Volume: 50 emails per day minimum
List source: Public optometry directories,
  state licensing boards, AOA member directory,
  Google Maps searches
DO NOT use Weave customer data (legal risk)

Email sequence:
Day 1: Value-focused intro
Day 4: Follow-up with data point
Day 8: Case study or insight
Day 14: Final breakup email

Subject lines that work:
"Your patients have $2.4B in expiring benefits"
"Most practices miss this Q4 revenue"
"How [similar practice] recovered $28K in 90 days"
