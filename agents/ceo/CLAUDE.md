## Context on Prism
Prism's biggest differentiator is the benefits reminder + SMS campaign feature. No one else in the optometry space makes it this easy for practices to proactively reach patients when their benefits are about to expire or reset. This is the wedge — insurance eligibility is the table stakes that gets us in the door, but campaigns are what keeps practices paying.

- Evaluating partnerships or integrations
