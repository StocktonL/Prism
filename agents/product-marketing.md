## Product

**Name:** Prism (marketed as Prizm at prizmvision.com)

**Core pitch:** "Year-round patient campaigns that drive optical revenue."
**Primary CTA message:** "Come spend your unused vision benefits on glasses or contacts."
**Live URL:** https://prizmvision.com
**Founding offer page:** https://prizmvision.com/founding

## What It Does

Prism identifies which patients have unused vision insurance benefits (exact dollar
amounts per patient), then generates and sends personalized campaigns that bring
those patients back in to spend their benefits before they expire.

**Key differentiator:** Exact dollar amounts in every message.
CORRECT: "You have $150 in frame benefits expiring Dec 31"
WRONG: "You have unused benefits available"

**Not a recall tool.** Not a generic patient communication tool. Not competing with Weave.
Prism is the campaign automation layer that adds insurance intelligence to whatever
the practice already uses.

## Target Customer

**Buyer:** Practice manager or practice owner (NOT the OD doctor, NOT front desk staff)
**Company type:** Independent in-network optometry practices, 1-3 doctors, 3-8 staff
**Company size signal:** 1-10 employees in Apollo
**Active patients:** 1,500-3,000 per practice
**Geography:** US only — starting Utah, Idaho, Nevada, Arizona

**Buyer persona:**
- Usually 30-55 years old
- Not tech-savvy
- Extremely time-constrained
- Measured on revenue and efficiency
- Already paying $300-800/month for Weave or similar tools
- Familiar with the pain of manual benefit verification

**What they care about:**
1. "How much additional revenue will this generate?"
2. "How much work does my staff have to do?"
3. "How long until we see results?"
4. "What does it cost vs what do we get back?"
